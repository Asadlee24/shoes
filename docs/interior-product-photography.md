## ImageGen instructions

Mode: edit. Each call uses the corresponding original photograph as image 1 and `public/products/taupe-gallery-clean-v1.webp` as image 2, for the backdrop and lighting only. The shared prompt below is followed by the product-specific instruction.

### Shared prompt

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset: professional ecommerce PRODUCT GALLERY photo for Qadam Flex. Image 1 is the actual product photo to edit. Image 2 is ONLY a backdrop, lighting and photographic quality reference; NEVER copy its brown shoe design onto the target. Retouch image 1 into a premium clean studio photograph. Keep the EXACT same shoes, shoe count, original camera angle, direction, relative arrangement, toe/heel shape, proportions, panel layout, stitching, laces, hardware, visible existing logos/lettering and true material colors. Preserve a pair as a pair; preserve an overhead view as overhead, side view as side, front view as front. Change only the environment, exposure and photographic clarity. Remove shop clutter, wires, taped cardboard, walls/edges, dirty improvised background, clear plastic cup supports, packaging/tissue stuffing, hands and hanging price tags/strings. Never remove shoelaces or actual product trim. Seamless neutral warm light-gray studio background near #e5e1dc, soft realistic contact shadows and neutral balanced studio lighting, detailed natural leather/suede/textile without plastic-looking overprocessing. Keep full shoes comfortably inside a portrait 4:5 frame with about 6-10% breathing room and no wasteful huge empty areas. Where source clips a small heel/toe edge, extend minimally using the same shoe's own geometry. No redesigned product, no extra shoe, no invented logo or text, no UI, watermark, border, people or props. 
```
